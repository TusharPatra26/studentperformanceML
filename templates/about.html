How we compared models

We trained seven regression models, tuned selected hyperparameters with randomized search, and compared their R² scores against the same 20% held-out test split.

Why this model

Linear Regression gave the strongest result in the current evaluation. Its simple, interpretable form is also a useful baseline for this student-score prediction project.

Use the estimate thoughtfully

The score is an estimate based on patterns in the available dataset. It should support exploration—not replace educator judgment or be treated as a guarantee.
